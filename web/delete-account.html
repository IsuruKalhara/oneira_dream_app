Delete your Dreamlore account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, from inside the app or by writing to us.

From inside the app

  1. Open Dreamlore.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. This cannot be undone.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Email support@bitfuzed.com from the address you signed in with, asking us to delete your Dreamlore account. We will confirm once it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address and display name held by Firebase Authentication) — deleted permanently.
  • Every dream, interpretation and picture stored on the device you are using — deleted permanently.
  • Anonymous rate-limit counters on our server — these expire on their own within about 40 days and are not linked to your identity.

Your dreams are stored on your device, not on our servers, so deleting the app also removes them from that device. If you used Dreamlore on more than one device, delete the app on each.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Dreamlore Plus is billed by Google Play. Cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions before deleting your account, or you will continue to be charged.

Retention

Account deletion takes effect immediately. Backups held by our providers are overwritten on their own schedules and are not used to restore deleted accounts. We do not keep a copy of your dreams.
