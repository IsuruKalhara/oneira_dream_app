What Dreamlore is

Dreamlore is a dream journal app by Bitfuzed for Android. It transcribes a spoken dream on the device, returns an interpretation grounded in public-domain dream literature with verbatim quotes, can generate a picture of the dream, and stores the journal only on the phone. It is free to start; Dreamlore Plus is a monthly or yearly subscription, the yearly plan with a 3-day free trial.

Questions people ask

What does Dreamlore quote from?

Public-domain dream literature: Sigmund Freud's The Interpretation of Dreams and Dream Psychology, and Gustavus Hindman Miller's Ten Thousand Dreams Interpreted. Every quote is checked word-for-word against the source before it is shown.

Is my dream journal private?

Yes. Your voice is transcribed on your phone and the audio never leaves it. The journal is stored only on your device. Dream text is sent transiently to an AI provider to produce the reading and is not stored by Dreamlore.

Can Dreamlore make a picture of my dream?

Yes. Dreamlore Plus paints a picture from your own description of the dream and saves it with the journal entry.

How much does Dreamlore cost?

The free tier includes a limited number of readings. Dreamlore Plus is a monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play; the yearly plan starts with a 3-day free trial and can be cancelled any time.

Is Dreamlore medical or psychological advice?

No. Dreamlore is for reflection and journaling. It is not medical, psychological, or predictive advice, and is not a substitute for professional care.
